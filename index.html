How a night goes

Back Room Hold'em, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Sit down

    You bring $1,000 to a table with three mobsters. Lean in with E to tip up the corners of your cards, or press V to look straight down at the felt.

  2. 2

    Take what they offer

    Every few hands, someone offers you something. A whiskey from the bartender. A cigar from Frankie. Or a round of Russian roulette with the Don: load one to five bullets, and if you live, your money grows. The more bullets, the more it grows.

  3. 3

    Cash out

    Get up whenever you like. The night comes back on one screen: your money hand by hand, and how the family feels about what you took from them. Win big and they get less friendly.

  4. 4

    Spend it on your bar

    Half of what you walk out with goes into your own bar. The other half comes back to the table the next night. Stools, a neon sign, a jukebox, a pool table. None of it earns a cent. It's just yours.
